\section{Scope and evidence status}
This supplement reports the locked V6 confirmation package, its historical
provenance, and a separately labelled independent P1 confirmation appendix.
V6 pooled inference used 40 paired seed blocks, each averaging the 16
task $\times$ split $\times$ model environments equally. Environment rows are
heterogeneity diagnostics. No new contrast, endpoint, exclusion, environment
selection, or statistical analysis was introduced into locked V6 inference.
